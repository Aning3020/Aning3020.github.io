探讨巧劲儿

老胡说

对的，然后你能把 leverage，leverage 这个部分再更具体和重点地说一说吗？

这个部分确实是我之前欠缺的，然后就是它是就有点像你事半功倍，你出一份力能有十份的结果，就是它有一个巧劲儿，巧妙的那个劲儿。

ChatGPT 说

Yes — leverage is exactly the “巧劲” part.

It means you are not relying only on your own raw effort. You are using position, timing, information, relationships, tools, systems, and existing momentum so that a small action can create a much larger result.

A simple definition could be:

Leverage = using one unit of effort to create many units of movement.

The opposite is brute force:

I push harder.
I work longer.
I endure more.
I do everything myself.

Leverage asks a different question:

Where can I apply the smallest effective force?

That is why it feels closer to “事半功倍.” Sometimes it is even 一分力，十分结果.

For you, leverage could become very concrete in a few ways:

  • Information leverage: one conversation with the right local person saves you two weeks of random searching.
  • Relationship leverage: instead of asking twenty strangers, one trusted person introduces you to three useful people.
  • Positioning leverage: being in Bangkok puts you close to English, sports, international networks, schools, coaches, and regional opportunities all at once.
  • Tool leverage: AI, automation, translation, research tools, databases, social media — one person can now do work that used to require a small team.
  • Reputation leverage: once people know what you are good at, opportunities start coming toward you instead of you chasing everything.
  • System leverage: you build a repeatable process once, then reuse it instead of solving the same problem from zero every time.
  • Capital leverage: money buys time, mobility, access, training, and better environments. The money itself is not the goal; the options it creates are.
  • Momentum leverage: when something is already moving, you join it instead of creating movement from nothing.

The most important part is that leverage is not “being clever” in a manipulative sense. It is more like seeing structure.

A person with leverage looks at a difficult situation and asks:

Where is the existing flow?
Who already knows this?
What resource is underused?
Which single move unlocks several other moves?
What can I build once and use repeatedly?
What should I absolutely not spend my energy on?

That last question matters a lot.

Because leverage is also about refusing low-return effort.

You may be especially suited to this because you are perceptive and quick. Your weakness in the past may have been that once you saw something important, you still tried to carry too much of it with your own body and willpower.

The next stage is:

see clearly → find the pivot point → apply force there

Very much like sport.

In tennis, leverage is not hitting every ball with maximum strength. It is timing, angle, body chain, placement, reading the opponent, using their pace, and choosing the right ball to attack.

Same in life.

Don’t generate all the force yourself.
Learn where force already exists, and how to redirect it.

That may become one of your biggest upgrades. 🌿

这就是你说的那个巧劲儿。
不是更用力。
是找到几个支点，然后轻轻一推，整个局开始动。
确实比较妙，互相作用降低难度，迁一发动发全身

比如9月十号左右，你只有七千，，如果把这件事真的当成一个 3–5 天的游戏关卡，以前会只有“拼命凑 8,000 元”这一条线。

而真正会用 local reading + leverage 的人，会同时动 钱、房、关系、时间 四个杠杆，让它们互相降低难度。

老胡讨论过程中的一些涌现：

最后，
其实我听下来不在于你钱多钱少。
你钱再多，你匹配那个钱的量级，你可能还是有那种钱不够的地方。

所以它更多的是一个，可能在你一个资本有一点，但是不完全够的时候，你怎么去聪明地去，还是，有不同的思路，然后让自己去能够也能拿到好的资源去生存下去。

比如：泰勒·斯威夫特，她不也遇到版权问题吗，然后什么邓紫棋啦，什么这个青峰啦，这些，就是你后来出名了，也会遇到挑战，并不只是钱的事儿。

感觉其实还是因为各种原因，钱可以兑换成一些这种生存智慧、街头智慧、生存技能，还有当地人际关系网络，在我这个阶段，它不够。在不够的时候，我能想到的最快的其实只有钱。

你钱多了后，你两三万去租房子，和你只有七千去租房子，这个战术、重点和打开方式确实是不一样的，但是现状就是这么个现状，确实咱们不是一个大资本，至少这个当下不是一个大资本进场的这么一个打法。人手也不多。

那就是用这个巧劲儿，活络的，甚至街头的方式来去打开试试看。
